# Info needed for the wedding site

Everything below is a `{{PLACEHOLDER}}` in `config.js`. Placeholders show highlighted yellow on the page until replaced.

## Basics
- Hashtag (if any)
- Contact email and optional phone
- RSVP deadline, and whether you want an RSVP form link (Google Forms, Zola, etc.) or email replies

## Story
- How you met
- First date or early memory
- The proposal story (beach photo details: where, when)

## Events
- Rehearsal time, dinner venue name, address, who is invited
- Ceremony: arrival time, doors open, parking, dress notes, any Mass or program details
- Reception: start and end time, food and drinks, dancing, indoor or outdoor at Meadowbrook Park
- Anything on Sunday (brunch, send-off)?

## Wedding party
- For each person: full name, role, side, one-line bio, optional photo
- Officiant, readers, flower girl and ring bearer, parents to list

## Travel and stay
- Nearest airport and driving notes
- Hotel name, room block code, booking link and cutoff date
- Parking at the church and at the park
- Shuttle between ceremony and reception?

## Guest questions
- Dress code
- Kids welcome or not
- Plus-one policy
- Rain plan
- Unplugged ceremony or photo policy

## Registry
- Registry names and links, or a message if you prefer no gifts

## Extras
- More photos (engagement session pics are great)
- Want a song request field, a guestbook, or a live-stream link?
